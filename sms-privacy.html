SMS privacy notice

Updated October 26, 2026

Gibbs TD Chase is operated by Sasha Bates, doing business as Clipboard Stats, and sends automated alerts about Jahmyr Gibbs's regular-season touchdowns. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel or HELP for help.

Website signup sends a confirmation text. Reply YES within 15 minutes to activate your subscription before receiving touchdown alerts. Any requests saved before SMS signup opened are retained for up to 90 days and do not activate a subscription. Contact sashacbates@gmail.com to cancel a saved request.

Information used for alerts

Only when you select the optional SMS checkbox and submit a signup request do we save your phone number and consent. Leaving the checkbox unchecked does not submit your number to our alert service. Our messaging provider processes message contents and delivery metadata. Our subscription database stores your number, signup method, signup and confirmation times, subscription status, terms version, and message identifiers and delivery results. We use this information to confirm subscriptions, send requested alerts, honor opt-outs, and prevent duplicate messages.

The web form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to prevent automated abuse. Cloudflare processes browser and network information for this security check. We store keyed hashes of your IP address and phone number in short-lived rate-limit records, retained for up to 48 hours plus the next cleanup cycle. We retain web signup request records for 90 days. Submitting the form sends a confirmation text; enrollment only occurs after a YES reply within 15 minutes.

Service providers

Twilio handles text delivery and Cloudflare hosts our alert service and subscription database. Information is shared with service providers only as needed to operate the service. Mobile numbers, SMS opt-in data, and consent are not sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Retention and your choices

Your active subscription remains until you unsubscribe or the service ends. Our routine cleanup removes unconfirmed signup records after seven days, stopped subscription records after 90 days, and individual alert delivery records after 90 days. Provider logs and backups may have separate retention periods. Reply STOP to end alerts. For access or deletion requests, contact Clipboard Stats at sashacbates@gmail.com.

Website analytics

The homepage uses Google Analytics to understand website visits. We do not send SMS subscriber numbers or message contents to Google Analytics.
